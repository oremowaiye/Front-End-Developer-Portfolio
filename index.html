What I do

I write and maintain automated tests for web applications. That means building page objects, keeping suites stable as the product changes, and investigating failures so they point at real defects rather than flaky selectors.

I came to testing from development, which shapes how I work: I read the application code, not just the UI, and I care about test suites being maintainable rather than merely passing.
